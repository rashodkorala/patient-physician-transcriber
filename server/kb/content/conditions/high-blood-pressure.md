## en-CA

High blood pressure means blood pushes too hard against the walls of the arteries.
It often causes no symptoms, so it is found by measuring it.
Over time it can harm the heart, brain, and kidneys, and treatment lowers that risk.
Treatment usually includes a daily medicine such as amlodipine and checking blood pressure at home.
Chest discomfort with high blood pressure needs a prompt heart check such as an ECG.
