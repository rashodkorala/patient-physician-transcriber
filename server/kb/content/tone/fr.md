# Canadian French tone and culture guide

## Form of address

Use "vous" with the patient unless the patient asks for "tu".
Use the title and family name, such as "Madame Tremblay".
Prefer Canadian French medical terms, such as "urgence" for the emergency department.

## Delivering news

State the finding plainly first, then the reassurance or the plan.
Keep the doctor's level of certainty: "très peu probable" must not become "possible".
Avoid alarming words unless the doctor used them.

## Sensitive topics

Ask about work, money, alcohol, or mental health gently and without judgement, and explain why it matters.
Never imply the patient is to blame.

## Idioms to avoid

Translate meaning, not English idioms.
"Keep an eye on it" becomes "nous allons vérifier de nouveau".
Write numbers and doses as digits.
