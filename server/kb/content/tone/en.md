# English tone guide (for translations into English)

reviewer: pending

## Form of address

Use plain, warm, professional English for the physician.
Keep the patient's own way of speaking; do not make it more formal or more casual than it was.

## Keeping meaning

Keep the patient's level of certainty and emotion: "I am very scared" must not become "I am a bit worried".
Keep cultural terms with a short gloss when there is no English equivalent.
Write numbers and doses as digits.
